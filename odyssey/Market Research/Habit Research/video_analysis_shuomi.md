# Video Analysis: "5 Best Apps to Organize Your Life and Work (That Aren't Notion or Obsidian)"
## Channel: ShuOmi | YouTube: https://youtu.be/YOh5lCY70is

---

## Video Summary

The video reviews 5 productivity apps aimed at people who find Notion and Obsidian too complex.
This is directly relevant to Odyssey since Odyssey is also positioning itself as a focused,
non-overwhelming productivity tool (the user explicitly said "I don't want to make it like
Notion or Obsidian").

### Apps Covered in the Video
1. TimeStripe — Multi-horizon life planning (daily → decade → lifetime)
2. Twos — Atomic daily notes + auto-rollover to-dos
3. Amplenote — Note-taking + task management hybrid
4. Anytype — Simpler Notion alternative for individuals
5. Heptabase — Visual knowledge management (canvas/mind-map based)

## Feature-by-Feature Analysis vs Odyssey

---

### 1. TimeStripe — "Horizons" Multi-Timeframe Planning

**What TimeStripe does:**
- Lets users plan across multiple time horizons simultaneously: Day / Week / Month / Year / Decade / Life
- "Climbs" feature: structured paths to build habits and reach goals progressively
- Breaks big goals into subgoals with daily actions
- Kanban boards for project management
- Community template gallery

**Core USP:** Bridges the gap between daily tasks and long-term life goals — you see *why* today's
habits matter in the context of your 5-year or lifetime vision.

---

#### Overlap with Odyssey (Already Exists)

| TimeStripe Feature | Odyssey Equivalent | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Daily planning view | Day Schedule / Planner page | BUILT |
| Habit tracking | Habits page with period-based scheduling | BUILT |
| Streak + progress tracking | Streak system, XP, ranks | BUILT |
| Journey timeline (day-by-day progress) | Journey page | BUILT |

---

#### What Odyssey Doesn't Have From TimeStripe

**★ LONG-TERM GOAL HORIZONS (Multi-timeframe planning)**

TimeStripe's biggest differentiator is connecting daily actions to bigger goals.
Odyssey tracks habits day-by-day but has no concept of:
- Weekly goals ("this week I want to complete 5 workouts")
- Monthly themes ("this month: build a sleep routine")
- Yearly ambitions ("this year: run a 5K")
- Life goals that cascade down to daily habits

This is the single most valuable feature gap identified from the video.
Users who ask "why am I doing this daily?" need a higher-level anchor.

**How Odyssey can adapt this (without becoming Notion):**
- Add a "Goals" section — simple 1-2 line goal statements at Week / Month / Year level
- Each habit can optionally be linked to a goal ("This habit contributes to: [Monthly Goal]")
- Stats page shows progress toward linked goals alongside daily streaks
- The wallpaper could optionally display the current weekly goal as a subtitle text

**★ COMMUNITY TEMPLATE GALLERY**
TimeStripe has user-submitted templates. This connects to our B10 (Habit Template Library)
but with a social/community layer — browsing what others built.
- Can start as a curated internal library (B10), then open to community submissions later.

---

### 2. Twos — Atomic Daily Notes with Auto-Rollover

**What Twos does:**
- Every piece of information is a "thing" (note, task, event, list item)
- Daily page that auto-creates each morning with unfinished items rolled over
- Tasks not done today automatically carry forward to tomorrow
- No manual "move to tomorrow" required
- Wear OS sync, calendar integration
- Free with no paywall on core features

**Core USP:** Zero friction daily capture — you never lose a task because it automatically
follows you to the next day until it's done.

---

#### Overlap with Odyssey (Already Exists)

| Twos Feature | Odyssey Equivalent | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Daily tasks | Habits scheduled per day | BUILT |
| Streak/progress tracking | Streak system | BUILT |
| Evening reminder | Evening Reminder Modal | BUILT |

---

#### What Odyssey Doesn't Have From Twos

**★ AUTO-ROLLOVER FOR UNCOMPLETED HABITS**

In Twos: if you don't complete a task today, it automatically appears tomorrow.
In Odyssey: if you miss a habit, it's just marked as missed and the day moves on.

This is a meaningful behavior difference. The auto-rollover approach:
- Removes the guilt of "I failed today" — the habit just moves forward
- Makes the system feel like a persistent companion rather than a scorekeeper
- Ties directly into non-punitive design (our B4)

**How Odyssey can adapt:**
- For non-time-bound habits (e.g., "Read for 30 min" without a fixed hour), allow rollover
- "Flexible habits" vs "Fixed habits" — fixed habits belong to a specific hour,
  flexible habits roll over to the next available slot if missed
- On the wallpaper, a "rolled over" habit could show with a different indicator

**★ FAST DAILY CAPTURE (Atomic "Things" System)**
Twos excels at quick, frictionless capture of any thought before it's lost.
Odyssey is structured around scheduled habits — you can't quickly add a one-off note or
thought without creating a full habit entry.

**How Odyssey can adapt:**
- "Quick capture" input — a single text field accessible from the header/wallpaper
  where users can dump a thought, task, or reminder. It lands in an "inbox" section.
- This feeds into B11 (Goal-Based Onboarding) and is related to B10 (Template Library)

---

### 3. Amplenote — Task + Note Hybrid with "Jot Mode"

**What Amplenote does:**
- Combines note-taking with task management in the same document
- "Jot Mode" — a minimal, distraction-free quick capture mode
- Notes can contain tasks that integrate with a calendar
- "Task Score" system prioritizes tasks by urgency + importance + time needed

**Core USP:** The same note can contain both written thoughts AND actionable tasks —
removing the friction between "thinking about" and "doing."

---

#### Overlap with Odyssey (Already Exists)

| Amplenote Feature | Odyssey Equivalent | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Task scheduling | Hour-based habit scheduling | BUILT |
| Progress analytics | Stats page | BUILT |

---

#### What Odyssey Doesn't Have From Amplenote

**★ TASK IMPORTANCE / PRIORITY SCORING**
Amplenote's "Task Score" automatically surfaces the most important tasks based on
deadline urgency × importance weight × time investment required.

Odyssey treats all habits equally — no concept of priority or urgency.

**How Odyssey can adapt (lightweight version):**
- Allow habits to be tagged as "Critical / Important / Optional"
- On the wallpaper, Critical habits get a highlighted border or accent color
- In the planner, sort/display habits with priority indicators

**★ JOT MODE (Ultra-fast single thought capture)**
Amplenote's "Jot Mode" is a single tap → type → done experience.
This overlaps with Twos' atomic capture concept.

Already noted under Twos above — this reinforces that QUICK CAPTURE is a
major validated gap in Odyssey.

---

### 4. Anytype — Personal Knowledge Base (Simpler Notion)

**What Anytype does:**
- A local-first, privacy-focused alternative to Notion
- Organizes information as "objects" with types and relations
- Supports pages, databases, kanban, calendars
- Fully offline, end-to-end encrypted

**Core USP:** All the power of Notion but offline-first, privacy-first, and less overwhelming.

---

#### Overlap with Odyssey
Minimal — Anytype is fundamentally a note-taking and knowledge base tool.
Odyssey is a habit + schedule app. There is very little direct overlap.

#### What Odyssey Can Note
Anytype's success confirms that users actively want:
- **Privacy-first, local-first data** — no cloud lock-in
- **Offline functionality** — the app works without internet

Odyssey uses Dexie.js (IndexedDB) which is already offline-first and local.
This is already a strength — it should be communicated as a feature, not hidden.

**Action:** Add "Your data stays on your device" messaging in the app's onboarding
and profile/settings page. This is a trust signal, especially for a habit/schedule app
that tracks personal daily behaviors.

---

### 5. Heptabase — Visual Canvas / Mind-Map Knowledge Management

**What Heptabase does:**
- Organizes notes visually on a canvas (spatial, drag-and-drop)
- Notes connect to each other with visible relationships
- Best for learning, research, and non-linear thinking
- "Whiteboards" for visual brainstorming

**Core USP:** Spatial thinking instead of hierarchical lists — see how ideas connect visually.

---

#### Overlap with Odyssey
Zero meaningful overlap. Heptabase is a knowledge management / research tool.
Odyssey is a habit/schedule app. These are different categories.

#### What Odyssey Can Note
Heptabase's canvas-style visualization is what the user explicitly said they do NOT want.
Confirmed: Odyssey should stay focused on the habit + time-schedule space.

The video mentioning Heptabase as a top-5 app actually validates Odyssey's direction —
the market is clearly hungry for focused tools that do one thing extremely well.

## New Features to Add to Feature Lists (from video)

The following are NEWLY IDENTIFIED features not previously in our lists.
These should be added to feature_lists.md as Standard features.

---

### NEW-B22 — Long-Term Goal Horizons
**What:** A simple "Goals" section where users write 1-2 sentence goals at Week / Month / Year
level. Each goal is a soft anchor that gives daily habits context and purpose.
**NOT a Notion clone** — no pages, no databases, no blocks. Just 3 text fields: this week,
this month, this year. Optional.
**Wallpaper integration:** The current weekly goal can optionally appear as a subtitle line
on the wallpaper card — "Working toward: Run a 5K"
**Effort:** Low-Medium
**Inspired by:** TimeStripe Horizons

---

### NEW-B23 — Habit-to-Goal Linking
**What:** When creating or editing a habit, optionally link it to one of the user's active goals.
The Stats page then shows "X% of your habits are contributing to active goals."
On the wallpaper, linked habits show a subtle goal indicator icon.
**Effort:** Low (once Goals exist from NEW-B22)
**Inspired by:** TimeStripe subgoals

---

### NEW-B24 — Flexible Habits with Auto-Rollover
**What:** A new habit type alongside the existing fixed-hour habits: "Flexible habits" have
no fixed time slot — they just need to be done sometime today. If missed, they automatically
roll over to tomorrow without breaking the streak (with a small "rolled over" indicator).
**Effort:** Medium — new habit type in DB, rollover logic in scheduler
**Inspired by:** Twos auto-rollover, also addresses non-punitive design (B4)

---

### NEW-B25 — Quick Capture Input
**What:** A floating or header-accessible single-line input field that lets users instantly
dump a habit idea, task, or thought. It lands in a lightweight "Inbox" section in the
Habits page. From there, the user can promote it to a real scheduled habit or delete it.
**Effort:** Low-Medium
**Inspired by:** Twos "things", Amplenote Jot Mode, Todoist quick add

---

### NEW-B26 — Habit Priority Tags (Critical / Important / Optional)
**What:** A simple 3-level priority tag on each habit. On the wallpaper, Critical habits get
a colored accent border. In the planner, habits are visually sorted by priority within each hour.
**Effort:** Low (new field + UI indicator)
**Inspired by:** Amplenote Task Score (simplified for habit context)
